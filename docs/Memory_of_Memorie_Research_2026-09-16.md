# Memory of Memorie: A Chill Story — competitor notes (2026-09-16)

Owner asked: "a newer game that reads like a 1:1 copy of Chill with You — what is it, what can we learn,
how is it relevant." Research done from the Steam store/reviews (EN + zh-CN), Famitsu, the BeXide
official site, Nintendo Everything, Metacritic, SteamSpy. Companion to
`CWYL_Xiaoheihe_Feedback_2026-08-17.md` and `AI_Companionship_Market_Research_2026-08.md`.

## What it is

| | |
|---|---|
| Title | Memory of Memorie: A Chill Story（中文名：梅莫莉：治愈物语） |
| Studio | BeXide Inc. (Japanese studio; dev + publisher) |
| Release | 2026-07-22 (Steam) / 07-23 (Switch, Switch 2 Edition) |
| Price | ¥1,200 / HK$72 (Switch 2 Edition ¥1,500); OST DLC separate |
| Languages | JA / EN / zh-CN / zh-TW / KO — **full audio in all five** |

| Owners | SteamSpy bucket 0–20k (so roughly 5–15k copies on Steam; Switch unknown) |
| Critic | Metacritic has one review: Nindie Spotlight 54 — "concept implemented reasonably well … lacking in overall ambition and depth" |

Pitch: you work at your desk while Memorie, an apprentice witch from another world, studies magic beside
you. Focus sessions (1-hour mode, or a 35-min Pomodoro of 15+5 cycles) accumulate; chapters unlock from
accumulated focus; her room and village change; she has "secrets she keeps hidden"; Celtic original
music and ambience; a calendar of your focus history; 15 achievements with timestamps.

## Is it a copy of Chill with You?

Not in the rip-off sense. It is the second entrant in the "work-with-me ADV" genre CWYL created, and
the two are on friendly terms: Steam sells an official **"Chill with You × Memory of Memorie" bundle**
(CWYL by Nestopi Inc., MoM by BeXide) with an extra 10% off. Players compare them constantly
("是聪音赢了" is a common zh-CN verdict), but the format — timer gates story, girl at a desk, lo-fi
room — is now a genre template, not one game's property. Third entrant confirms the genre is real.

## What players say (Steam EN + zh-CN)

Praise
- Memorie's voice/animation, camera angles, weather shuffle ("improvement over CWYL" on presentation).
- Story "earned instead of handed to you": unlocks by focus only, **no currency/paywall** — zh-CN
  reviews explicitly call this 人性化 vs. the CWYL grind-hour complaints.
- Achievement medals with timestamps; calendar of focus history (visible progress).
- Music good enough that "just launching it and leaving it running is soothing".
- Works for ADHD / stressed users as a focus tool.

Complaints (the useful part)
- **"做了一半就放出来了吗？没有todo，没有日程，没有记事本"** — no to-do list, schedule, notes, habit
  tracker. Feature floor for the genre now includes a to-do list.
- Thin content at launch: "所有的装饰品都是剧情解锁，场景环境做的很少", only two ambient tracks, few
  outfits, "many teacups but few teapots".
- Slow unlocks; 10 hours of pomodoros before customization/story feel deep.
- Launch audio was distracting (ambient SFX too busy) — patched later; music-import eats RAM.
- **Chinese localization criticized as inadequate** even with full Chinese voice.
- "底子可以，不过相比同类型竞品聪音来说，功能少一些"; "如果无聊了还有 Chill With You 或 gogh".
- Critic: no ambition/depth — a timer with a cute frame, not a story worth talking about.

## What this means for Project Chill

1. **Genre validated, bar raised on the frame, not the heart.** Two shipped titles, both praised for
   room/music/voice, both dinged for shallow characters and thin features. Neither has AI free chat,
   neither has a companion who actually *remembers* you, neither has a companion with her own arc that
   the player half-discovers. Our three differentiators still stand (Mandarin-first, 她记得你, Yua's own
   arc) — and the "secret she hides" device is now table stakes, so Yua's secret has to be *specific*
   (the novel, the 荐书卡, the notebook) rather than a generic "mystery".
2. **Mandarin-first is a real edge, again.** A Japanese studio shipped full zh-CN audio and still got
   "localization inadequate". Native-feeling Chinese lines (the whole arena/taste-log pipeline) is the
   thing neither competitor has.
3. **Feature floor to match before a public post:** a to-do list (ours exists — the 小本子), a calendar /
   history of focus sessions with timestamps (**we do not have this; cheap; players love it**), and
   achievement-style milestones. Don't ship without the calendar if we can help it.
4. **Unlock pacing:** MoM is punished for "slow"; CWYL for "grind". Our per-session episode + 15/30/60
   is faster than both; keep it. Intimacy gates on real focus seconds are fine as long as *something*
   new appears every session (the living notebook and 杂谈池 are exactly that filler).
5. **Presentation notes that landed with players:** camera angles, weather/window shuffle, timestamps
   on achievements, quiet ambience by default. We already have six window views (rain/clear/night/
   sunset/seaside/treetops) — surface them; keep ambient SFX sparse (MoM's launch complaint).
6. **Positioning line for 小红书/B站:** not "another CWYL" — "the one where she remembers what you said,
   and she has her own thing going on". The bundle proves the market treats these as a set; we want
   to be the one people mention as *different*, the way reviews mention "gogh" as the alternative.
7. **Platform thought (later):** MoM shipped Switch day-one at ¥1,200. Not for the demo; note for the
   Stage-2 plan.
